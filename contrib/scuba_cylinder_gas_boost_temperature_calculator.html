Thermodynamic Energy Balance & Physics Correction

1. Flow Work vs. Pure Enthalpy Input:

When filling a rigid container, net energy added to the gas is given by flow work $\dot{m} (C_p T_{\text{in}} - C_v T_{\text{tank}}) \approx \dot{m} R T_{\text{in}}$. This prevents over-counting gas heat capacity and correctly predicts realistic peak temperatures ($\sim 44^\circ\text{C}$ for 0→210 bar in 20m air fill).

2. Gas Mix & Material Capacity:

Monatomic Helium ($\gamma = 1.66$) lacks rotational degrees of freedom, generating higher flow work heat than Nitrogen or Oxygen ($\gamma = 1.40$). Steel walls heat faster due to lower heat capacity ($C = 490\text{ J/kg}\cdot\text{K}$) than AL80 aluminum ($C = 900\text{ J/kg}\cdot\text{K}$).
