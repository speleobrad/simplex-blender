About Simplex blender

Made with curiosity by Speleobrad.

Simplex blender plans gas fills around your leftover cylinders and the pressure you need in the water rather than a mixing station's menu, treating temperature as a blending parameter as important as composition, so you can see what the tank goes through while filling and reduce its thermal stress.
