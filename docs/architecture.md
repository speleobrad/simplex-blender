# Architecture

## Runtime assets

`index.html` owns the page markup, including the static New compressor and New booster controls, and relative asset references. `css/app.css` contains theme color variables for both appearances and custom table/scrollbar rules. The page loads Tailwind from its existing CDN, then `js/tailwind-config.js` maps utility colors to those variables, followed by the native-module entry `js/main.js`.

## JavaScript boundaries

- `js/main.js` owns browser event delegation, including equipment-clone behavior, editable bank state, settings drafts, preset-import file/URL loading and immediate persistence, active theme, selected tab, form parsing, supported-range validation, and calculation orchestration.
- `js/theme.js` loads and saves the explicit light/dark choice in browser `localStorage`; light is the default.
- `js/bank-storage.js` validates, loads, and saves the editable non-Air supply-bank list in browser `localStorage`; it migrates the previous built-in Helium/Tx 18/45/Oxygen list to the current defaults, discards legacy finite Air records, and allows an empty list.
- `js/settings.js` defines and validates the default gas/cylinder presets, persisted gas model, active flat booster and compressor fields, and named compressor/booster preset collections; it persists user-configured settings in browser `localStorage`, migrates legacy volume-only and equipment-collection settings, and reports when unreadable saved data falls back to defaults.
- `js/preset-import.js` normalizes gas, cylinder, and combined equipment JSON sources, validates candidates, applies category-specific duplicate policies (gas by content; cylinders and equipment by normalized name), and immutably merges selected new presets without DOM dependencies. Repository-compatible defaults live in `presets/`.
- `js/gas-input.js` parses slash-delimited O₂/He paste input and provides strict numeric parsing and gas-composition validation without DOM dependencies.
- `js/input-validation.js` enforces supported calculator ranges and supply-bank record constraints, shared by the UI, optimizer, and persistence layer.
- `js/physics.js` contains pure gas-property functions, including compressibility-factor calculation from an already-known density; it has no DOM dependency.
- `js/thermodynamics.js` simulates configured-rate draining as well as transfer rates, numerically integrates signed pressure-change heating/cooling with ambient heat loss, and applies post-transfer cooling waits for a completed protocol; it has no DOM dependency and returns thermal values alongside each step plus final transfer-end and ambient-settled estimates.
- `js/optimizer.js` validates the gas model, calculator ranges, user bank records, physical gas compositions, and thermal equipment input before constructing the requested-mix and maximum-pressure linear programs; it injects unlimited compressor Air, enforces the shared booster minimum source pressure, caps compressor and non-Air boost stages independently, rechecks recovered solver variables against the original constraints, applies the oxygen-transfer safety limit, and returns gas data, solver steps, protocol entries with source minimum/exhaustion metadata and oxygen-rich compression ratios, thermal estimates, and ternary trajectory points carrying each used source bank's starting and residual pressure.
- `js/onboarding.js` defines the validated, persisted consent and tour-completion state plus the ordered eight tour steps; this onboarding state is separate from calculation settings.
- `js/analytics.js` injects the Google Analytics tag once, and only after the visitor accepts consent.
- `js/ui/onboarding.js` renders the consent dialog and guided spotlight tour, handles its focus, keyboard, persistence, narrow-viewport, and reduced-motion behavior, and requests the tab required by each step.
- `js/ui/settings-dialog.js` renders the persisted gas-model choice, editable gas and cylinder preset lists, compressor and booster preset selectors, active equipment fields, the Preset management importer, and refreshes cylinder and gas choices.
- `js/ui/equipment-summary.js` renders the active compressor and booster names plus compact fill, boost, decant, and pressure-ceiling parameters below the supply-bank controls; unmatched active values are labeled Custom.
- `js/ui/storage-warning.js` renders persistence and rejected-bank-edit warnings from controller state.
- `js/ui/supply-banks.js` renders editable non-Air bank properties and gas-preset selectors; compressor Air is implicit and is not shown as a bank row, while derived N₂, Z, and minimum-pressure columns are not shown.
- `js/ui/results.js` renders gas metrics and the pumping protocol, including formatted Boost/Decant/Equalize/Top-up instructions, end-of-stage pressures/mixes, oxygen-rich compression ratios, source-end pressures and exhausted-source warnings, thermal rate/transfer-end/wait estimates, final hot and ambient-settled pressure summaries, and explicit infeasible feedback.
- `js/ui/thermal-charts.js` converts the completed thermal protocol into elapsed-time step-start, transfer-end, and ambient-settled points, then renders synchronized temperature and pressure SVG charts. Alternating bands span each displayed step's transfer-plus-wait interval, and their captions alternate between two rows; an accessible data table includes point times plus one rotated, three-row step label per displayed protocol step. Chart points and table rows share point indices and synchronize hover/focus highlighting. It includes drain preparation, omits the preserve-only no-op, and stops at the final ambient-settled state.
- `js/ui/ternary.js` shades the gray complementary N₂/Air/He region, draws dashed Air/O₂/He and full-simplex boundaries, reports composition under the pointer, populates the Tank summary tab's keyboard-operable O₂/He tables for reference mixes and trajectory data with source-bank start/end pressures, observes its container size, and bounds path-centered wheel and button zoom using the active theme's chart colors.

Calculation modules pass plain values to UI modules. UI renderers own only their assigned DOM surface. Bank controls are generated dynamically and handled through delegated events in `main.js`; bank names are treated as text. Preset edits remain transient drafts until the user saves settings, while confirmed preset imports immediately write the merged settings to `localStorage`; settings category selection is transient UI state. Onboarding consent and tour-completion state is stored separately from calculation settings, and accepting consent is the only path that loads the Analytics tag. Selecting a cylinder preset supplies the working tank's volume, material, mass, and pressure rating, while a gas-bank preset also updates that bank's displayed name.

## Data flow

1. The settings dialog edits the gas model, gas and cylinder presets, named equipment collections, and active equipment-setting drafts through transient category panes; category-specific clone actions append uniquely named copies of the active compressor or booster values without changing those active values. Saving validates and persists the draft in `localStorage`. Preset management loads JSON from a local file or editable online URL, previews valid new entries alongside disabled content/name duplicates and invalid gas name conflicts, and immediately persists confirmed selections through the same settings storage path. Legacy volume-only settings are normalized into cylinder presets when loaded. `bank-storage.js` loads saved non-Air bank records, removes legacy Air records, and writes user-bank mutations for the next session.
2. On first load, `main.js` reads the separate onboarding state. Without consent it opens the consent dialog and does not load Analytics. Accepting consent persists that state, injects the Analytics tag, and opens the tour; with consent but no completed tour it opens the tour directly. A completed tour is not reopened automatically, but the header wand re-enters it at Welcome. Tour navigation selects the tab required by each spotlight target.
3. `main.js` applies the saved theme to the root element; CSS variables update app colors, and the ternary canvas redraws after theme changes.
4. A form, bank, gas preset, cylinder preset, physics-setting, or main-screen tank thermal-input change causes `main.js` to collect one input object. The browser uses the selected cylinder preset for working-tank volume, material, mass, and working-pressure validation; it uses the saved gas model and maps ambient temperature to both `physics.temperatureC` and `thermal.ambientTemperatureC`; inlet gas temperature remains independent.
5. `optimizeBlend` injects unlimited 21/0 compressor Air, enforces the 232-bar default compressor ceiling and 230-bar default non-Air booster ceiling from settings, calculates gas quantities and bank availability, solves for the requested exact mix, then finds a maximum safe pressure recipe for that same mix if the requested pressure is unavailable. Transfer protocol entries identify non-Air sources that reach the shared booster minimum and include oxygen-rich source-end/destination compression ratios. Compressor Air may precede later non-Air stages above the compressor ceiling; a non-Air Boost stage never exceeds the booster ceiling.
6. `main.js` sends bank state to the bank renderer, active equipment settings to the equipment-summary renderer, and the optimizer result to the results, thermal-chart, and ternary renderers. The results renderer displays thermal step estimates, source-end warnings, and distinguishes the final transfer-end pressure from the settled ambient estimate.
7. Selecting the Diagram tab or resizing its container redraws the latest chart; its accessible data tables update with the same reference mixes and trajectory points. Selecting Thermal graphs shows synchronized temperature and pressure histories on a minutes-based X-axis, with alternating bands and alternating caption rows separating displayed protocol steps. Both histories include drain preparation, omit the preserve-only no-op, and stop at the final ambient-settled point; hovering or keyboard-focusing a point or table row highlights the matching entries across both charts and the table.

The app uses browser-native ES modules and has no bundling step. Serve the directory over HTTP; see [Development](development.md).
