# Blend Optimizer

`js/optimizer.js` validates the gas model, calculator ranges, user-managed non-Air bank records, physical gas compositions, and thermal equipment input before constructing the requested-mix and maximum-pressure linear programs. It injects unlimited 21/0 compressor Air, applies the shared booster minimum source pressure to finite banks, enforces the compressor and booster operation ceilings, and returns gas quantities, pumping steps, and ternary trajectory data. Per-bank available-mole limits remain internal to optimization.

## Pressure-limit policy

The persisted `pressureLimitMode` is **`hard-stop`** by default. Hard stop enforces the selected cylinder's working-pressure ceiling (with hot overfill only for above-ambient transients), automatically inserting cooling waits or splitting transfers when needed. **`warning`** mode bypasses only those cylinder working-pressure and hot-overfill checks; it does not bypass supported 350-bar bounds, the calculator's 10-bar target minimum, or compressor/booster pressure ceilings. Warning mode therefore introduces no cylinder-only wait; `main.js` collects pressure-limit observations from the returned calculation and thermal states and attaches them for visible result notices rather than making an over-limit fill safe.

Hot overfill applies only to above-ambient transient states. In Hard stop, ambient projections, settled states, and the target-temperature result remain at or below working pressure; in Warning, those outputs may exceed working pressure because the cylinder threshold is reported rather than enforced. The shared pressure-limit policy is propagated into both Optimize and Compare; equipment and calculator constraints remain active in either mode.

## Decision variables and constraints

- `x_cyl`: moles retained from the cylinder's initial mix.
- `x_i`: moles transferred from each enabled finite non-Air supply bank; the implicit compressor-Air variable is solver-only and has no persisted bank record.
- Total-mole equality: `x_cyl + Σ x_i = n_target`.
- Oxygen equality: `fO2_initial x_cyl + Σ fO2_i x_i = fO2_target n_target`.
- Helium equality: `fHe_initial x_cyl + Σ fHe_i x_i = fHe_target n_target`.
- Cylinder bound: `x_cyl ≤ n_initial`.
- Compressor Air is an implicit unlimited source. It is available without a bank row, uses the configured `maxCompressorPressureBar` ceiling, and is emitted as a `Top-up` operation rather than a reservoir transfer.
- Non-Air `Boost` stages may end no higher than `maxBoosterOutputPressureBar`. Non-Air `Decant` and `Equalize` stages may exceed that setting when source pressure supports them. Compressor Air may be staged before later non-Air transfers when a final target exceeds the compressor ceiling.

Compare receiver pressure inputs accept **0–350 bar**. This receiver range is distinct from the calculator target-pressure range, which remains **10–350 bar**.
- Legacy finite Air bank records are removed during persistence migration; user-managed bank capacity applies only to non-Air banks.

The solver adds artificial variables for the three equality constraints and performs two phases: phase 1 minimizes artificial-variable residue to test feasibility using a tolerance scaled to the LP right-hand-side magnitudes; phase 2 minimizes the configured gas-cost objective. Before accepting a solution, the optimizer rechecks the recovered variables against the original equality and capacity constraints. This prevents small absolute residuals or phase-two artificial-variable drift from hiding composition mismatches in low-inventory blends.

## Maximum-pressure fallback

If the requested pressure cannot be reached with the exact requested mix, a second LP maximizes total retained and transferred moles under the same cylinder and bank capacity bounds. If that inventory maximum is unavailable under the active constraints, the optimizer tries preferred-source, source-excluded, balanced, and adaptively refined pairwise oxygen-rich-bank allocations before searching lower exact-mix pressures. The selected real-gas model converts the maximum available amount to pressure. In Hard stop, cylinder limits make this a maximum safely achievable pressure for the cylinder policy; in Warning, it may exceed cylinder limits and is not a safety guarantee. Its fallback protocol drains every cylinder amount required by the exact mix, including pressure reductions below 0.5 bar. If no positive amount is possible, no fallback recipe is returned.

The optimizer compares requested component moles with gross component inventory in the starting cylinder and enabled banks above the shared booster minimum pressure. For a definite component shortage, the alert selects the enabled bank supplying the most of that component in the fallback recipe (or the bank with the greatest available component inventory) and converts the missing amount into the pressure required in that bank's volume and gas mix, using the selected model. It reports the bank's current pressure and the shared booster minimum as context; if no enabled bank contains the component, it says so instead of using an unrelated volume. When a required source density exceeds the van der Waals model's supported range, the UI states that no numeric pressure estimate is available rather than showing a sentinel.

## Objective and protocol

The normal retained-gas coefficient is `-5`, favoring gas already in the cylinder. Each supply-bank coefficient is `(He_fraction² × 100) + (O2_fraction × 0.4) + 1`. These weights are part of the existing model, not a market-price calculation. Safety-prioritized re-solves use a target-moles-scaled retained-gas coefficient that makes cylinder retention lexicographically primary; oxygen-rich source pressure selection, preferred-source attempts, and all other bank costs are secondary. A safety re-solve may discard working gas only when no safe recipe can retain it.

For a feasible solution, the protocol drains whenever the selected recipe requires venting, including pressure reductions below 0.5 bar; only pressure changes at floating-point roundoff scale are ignored. The retained/vented amounts and final pressure match the physical protocol stages. Non-Air supply transfers retain descending source O₂ ordering. Compressor Air is always labelled `Top-up`; it is placed before later non-Air transfers when the final target exceeds the compressor ceiling and otherwise remains the final Air stage. A transfer is labeled `Boost` when source pressure is lower than the working tank at both ends, `Decant` when it is higher at both ends, and `Equalize` when it starts higher and ends equal. Non-Air `Boost` stages are capped by `maxBoosterOutputPressureBar`; decant/equalize stages remain source-pressure limited.
After each boost from a bank with more than 25% O₂, the optimizer checks that the receiving cylinder's resulting pressure is no more than four times the source bank's residual pressure. Both pressures use the selected gas model. A violation rejects the recipe with an error and no blend protocol. Each mixing stage includes its target pressure, source-gas leftover pressure, interim O₂/He mix, and—when relevant—the O₂ compression ratio. Helium vented is the vented gas volume (1 bar equivalent) times the working tank's initial helium fraction; supply gas is never vented. Helium salvaged includes helium retained from the working tank when its mix is below 98% He, plus helium supplied by source mixes below 98% He; mixes at 98% He or above are excluded.

## Thermal protocol estimates

When the main-screen thermal tank inputs are present, `js/optimizer.js` passes the completed static protocol to `js/thermodynamics.js`. The selected cylinder preset supplies the working tank's volume, material, and empty assembly mass; the simulation applies configured booster/compressor/decant rates, uses the configured inlet gas temperature, applies the signed booster or compressor temperature modifier for those equipment paths, integrates incoming-gas heating and convective heat loss during every positive pressure increase, and applies the selected material and cooling-medium time constant after each stage. Implicit compressor-Air filling is represented as a `Top-up` stage and uses compressor flow; it may be followed by non-Air decant or boost stages when their limits permit it. The initial `Preserve 100% of Cylinder Gas` step has no transfer and therefore no post-step wait. The final stage is solved so its post-cooling pressure matches the requested target at the configured target temperature; the thermal result reports both hot transfer-end and settled target-temperature estimates.

Cylinder materials and their thermal parameters live in `js/materials.js`. `carbon-wrapped-steel` (shown as "Fero-carbon", used for steel-lined, carbon hoop-wrapped cylinders such as the Worthington Carbondive) reuses the steel parameters unchanged. That choice is uncalibrated: the steel liner carries most of the mass, but no measured fill or cool-down data exists for the wrap, which may slow cool-down in water. An unknown material throws instead of falling back to steel; aluminium-lined and polymer-lined composites are unsupported.

Drain steps use the configured `drainingRateBarPerMin`, integrate the same signed energy balance with negative mass flow, and therefore cool while pressure falls; their pressure and duration are never allowed below 0 bar or 0 minutes.

The target temperature is a final settled-temperature requirement. It must not be below ambient; the separate wait target temperature remains applicable only to between-fill waits selected as `temperature`.

Each cylinder preset defines a working-pressure limit and a hot-overfill percentage (default 2%; valid values are 0–5% in 0.5% steps). Only actual transient states above ambient may use `workingPressureBar × (1 + hotOverfillPercent / 100)`. In Hard stop, actual ambient projections, the final ambient-settled pressure, and the final pressure projected to the requested target in-water temperature remain capped at working pressure; in Warning, those states may exceed working pressure and are reported as cylinder-limit observations. Interim measured readings use the hot limit only when their measured temperature is above ambient in Hard stop; Warning reports measured exceedances without cylinder enforcement. The selected pressure-limit mode determines whether these cylinder ceilings stop planning or become reported warnings; compressor and booster operation ceilings remain independent, and Compare Recipe target inputs remain subject to supported calculator bounds.


These are operational estimates, not a pressure guarantee: actual heating, transfer rate, and cooling depend on equipment condition, gas flow, fill technique, and the surrounding environment. The optimizer does not use the estimates to relax gas composition, supply capacity, equipment ceilings, or transfer-safety constraints.

If the requested pressure is unreachable, the optimizer reports the maximum pressure under the active non-cylinder constraints for the exact target mix and its recipe. In Hard stop this is bounded by the cylinder policy; in Warning it may exceed cylinder limits and is not a safety guarantee. If no positive amount exists, the UI explains why and shows no recipe.
